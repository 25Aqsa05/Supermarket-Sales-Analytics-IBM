Business Decisions & Recommendations

📦 Inventory & Stocking

Beverages is the top revenue category (₹56,108 · 22.95%). Increase shelf allocation and ensure supplier contracts guarantee consistent stock. Introduce premium and imported variants to boost the average unit price.

🏙️ City-Level Expansion

Mumbai (Branch C) generates ₹72,469 — the highest among all cities. Consider opening a second counter or a dedicated express lane. Targeted loyalty campaigns in Mumbai can further drive frequency.

💳 Payment Promotions

UPI accounts for ₹67,910 and the highest average basket (₹534.73). Negotiate lower MDR rates with the UPI provider and promote UPI-exclusive cashback offers to shift more transactions to this low-friction channel.

👤 Membership Programme

Members contribute 58.5% of revenue (₹1,43,009) despite being 59.2% of transactions, but Normal customers have a slightly higher avg basket (₹497). Create a tiered rewards programme to accelerate conversion of Normal → Member buyers.

⭐ Quality Improvement

Fruits has the lowest average rating (3.83/5) despite a reasonable ₹23,263 in sales. Audit freshness standards, improve display, and gather targeted customer feedback to lift the rating above 4.0.

📅 Seasonal Planning

April 2026 was the peak month (₹52,570) while February was the lowest full month (₹30,068). Pre-stock high-demand categories and run promotional campaigns in January–February to smooth the revenue curve.

🛍️ Upsell & Bundle Deals

The Quantity vs Sales scatter shows a strong positive correlation. Train floor staff to suggest bundle deals (e.g., Coffee + Bakery combo). Multi-unit discounts on Snacks and Bakery can raise their below-average basket values.

🏢 Branch Benchmarking

Branch A (Jaipur) has the lowest transaction count (105) but the second-highest average basket (₹498.64). Study traffic drivers at Branch C (Mumbai) and implement them in Jaipur to grow transaction volume without sacrificing basket size.
