# Supermarket Sales Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket sales data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

The project includes data cleaning, data quality checks, sales calculations, group-wise analysis, visualization, and business recommendations.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall supermarket sales performance
- Compare sales across product categories
- Analyze sales by city and branch
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Compare customer types
- Analyze payment methods
- Study customer ratings
- Generate business recommendations

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Plotly
- Streamlit
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## 📈 Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Sales by Category
- Sales by City
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Payment Method
- Sales by Category and Gender
- Branch × Category Analysis
- Top Products by Revenue
- Customer Rating Distribution
- Sales by Customer Type
- Quantity vs Sales

## 📌 Key Findings

- Total revenue analyzed: ₹2,44,411
- Total transactions: 500
- Average basket size: ₹488.82
- Average customer rating: 3.99/5
- Beverages generated the highest category revenue.
- Mumbai (Branch C) generated the highest city-level sales.
- UPI had the highest total sales among payment methods.
- April 2026 was the peak revenue month.
